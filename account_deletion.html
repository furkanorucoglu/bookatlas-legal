Account Deletion

Last updated: 13 November 2026

You can permanently delete your BookAtlas account and the data linked to it in either of the following ways.

Option 1: In the app

  1. Open BookAtlas.
  2. Go to your profile.
  3. Tap “Delete Account”.
  4. Enter your password to confirm.

Option 2: By email

If you cannot access the App, send an email with the subject “BookAtlas Account Deletion Request” to destek@bookatlas.info from the email address registered to your account, stating that address and your full name. Requests are processed within 7 business days after we verify your identity.

What is deleted

When your account is deleted, the following are removed permanently and cannot be restored:

  • your account and profile details (email, name, username, date of birth, profile photo);
  • all of your book entries and your world map;
  • your reading list and reading goals;
  • your AI book and movie recommendation history;
  • your statistics and sharing records;
  • your Premium membership record.

Deletion cannot be undone. Removing data completely from backup systems can take up to 30 days. Records we are legally required to keep may be retained for the relevant statutory period. You can create a new account with the same email address, but you will not have access to your old data.

If you have an active subscription: deleting your account does not cancel your store subscription. To avoid further charges, cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account settings before deleting your account.

If you want to keep your records: before deleting, use the export button on the Statistics page to download your book entries as a CSV file.
