From a finished book to a coloured map

Three steps, a few seconds each.

  1. Log a book

    Search by title, scan the barcode on the back cover, or type it in. Add the country, the city and a note if you like.

  2. Watch the map fill in

    Every book colours its country. The more you read from a place, the deeper the green.

  3. Find the next one

    Ask the assistant for a recommendation, or tap a country to explore its literature.

Alternative literature

The shelf beyond the bestsellers

BookAtlas grew out of a simple habit: recommending the writers who rarely make the front table. Translated fiction, small presses, regional voices, books that deserve more readers than they have.

The assistant follows the same line. It favours overlooked authors and places you have not read from yet — and if you just want a well-loved classic, ask and it will give you one.

Questions

Is BookAtlas free?

Yes. Logging books, the world map, the reading list, goals and statistics are free. Free accounts also get 3 AI recommendation credits per day. Premium raises that to 20.

Which languages does it support?

The app is available in English and Turkish, and it follows your device language. You can log books in any language.

How do the AI recommendations work?

When you ask for a recommendation, the titles, authors and countries of the books you have read are sent to an AI model, together with anything you type. Your name and email are not sent. Recommendations are generated by AI and may contain mistakes, so check a book before you buy it.

Do I need an account?

Yes. Your books are stored in your account so that they are there when you change phones.

How do I delete my account?

Open your profile in the app and choose Delete Account. See the Account Deletion page for details.
